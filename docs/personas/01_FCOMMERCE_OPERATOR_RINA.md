## 🎯 Goals & Motivations

### Primary Goals
1. **Increase Order Volume:** Grow from 8-10 orders/day to 20-25 orders/day
2. **Reduce Operational Friction:** Spend less time on manual tasks (order entry, courier booking)
3. **Improve Cash Flow:** Reduce unpaid deliveries and payment delays
4. **Scale Without Hiring:** Automate repetitive tasks to avoid hiring costs

### Secondary Goals
- Build customer loyalty through better communication
- Expand product range based on trending items
- Maintain work-life balance (family responsibilities)

### Emotional Drivers
- **Pride:** Building independent income while managing household
- **Security:** Financial stability for family's future
- **Recognition:** Social status as successful entrepreneur in community
- **Freedom:** Flexibility to work from home around family schedule

## 😫 Pain Points & Frustrations

### Critical Pain Points (Daily)
| Pain Point | Frequency | Impact | Current Workaround |
|------------|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| Manual order entry from FB comments/WhatsApp | 50+ times/day | High (time-consuming, error-prone) | Copy-paste to notebook, then to courier app |
| Courier coordination (Pathao/Steadfast) | 10-15 times/day | Medium (delays if missed) | Multiple phone calls, WhatsApp messages |
| Payment verification (bKash/Nagad) | 20+ times/day | High (cash flow risk) | Manual SMS checking, screenshot matching |
| Customer follow-ups for unpaid orders | 5-10 times/day | High (revenue loss) | Polite but persistent WhatsApp reminders |
| Inventory tracking | Daily | Medium (overselling risk) | Physical notebook, mental notes |

### Weekly Pain Points
- Reconciling weekly sales with bank/MFS statements (2-3 hours every Friday)
- Responding to customer complaints about delivery delays
- Researching new products/suppliers from Nilkhet or online

### Monthly Pain Points
- VAT/tax calculation and filing (confusing, fears penalties)
- Business performance analysis (no structured reporting)

## 📱 Technology Stack

### Daily Apps
| App | Usage | Pain Points |
|-----|-------|-------------|
| Facebook | 4-5 hours/day (customer interaction, posts) | Algorithm changes reduce organic reach |
| WhatsApp | 3-4 hours/day (orders, support) | No CRM, messages get lost |
| IMO | 1-2 hours/day (voice calls with customers) | Call quality issues |
| bKash/Nagad | 10-15 transactions/day | Manual verification, no auto-reconciliation |
| Pathao/Steadfast | 10-15 bookings/day | Separate apps, different interfaces |
| Calculator | Constant use | Switching between apps slows workflow |

### Desired Features
- **Unified Dashboard:** See all orders, payments, shipments in one place
- **Auto-Order Entry:** AI extracts order details from FB comments/WhatsApp messages
- **Smart Reminders:** Automated payment follow-ups with polite templates
- **Courier Comparison:** Best price/speed across Pathao, Steadfast, RedX
- **Inventory Alerts:** Low stock warnings before overselling

## 🔄 Daily Workflow

### Morning (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)
```
9:00 AM  → Check overnight FB comments/WhatsApp messages
9:30 AM  → Confirm orders, check inventory availability
10:00 AM → Verify payments (bKash/Nagad SMS)
10:30 AM → Book couriers for confirmed orders
11:00 AM → Pack morning orders, hand over to courier
11:30 AM → Post new product photos on FB/Instagram
```

### Afternoon (12:00 PM - 5:00 PM)
```
12:00 PM → Lunch break + family time
2:00 PM  → Respond to customer inquiries
3:00 PM  → Follow up on unpaid/delivery-failed orders
4:00 PM  → Source new products (online/Nilkhet visits)
5:00 PM  → Update inventory notebook
```

### Evening (7:00 PM - 10:00 PM)
```
7:00 PM  → Dinner + family time
9:00 PM  → Quick check of day's orders/payments
9:30 PM  → Plan next day's priorities
10:00 PM → Close apps, rest
```

## 💬 Language Patterns

### Common Phrases (Bangla/Banglish)
- "Order confirm koren" (Confirm the order)
- "Payment pathaisen?" (Have you sent payment?)
- "Delivery charge ৳ 60" (Delivery charge is 60 Taka)
- "Stock e nai" (Out of stock)
- "Kalke pathabo" (Will send tomorrow)
- "bKash number e pathan" (Send to bKash number)
- "Tracking number den" (Give me tracking number)

### Communication Style
- **Polite but direct:** Uses "apu/dada" (sister/brother) for rapport
- **Emoji-heavy:** 😊👍✅ for positive reinforcement
- **Urgency markers:** "Jaldi din" (Give quickly), "Aajkei pathaben" (Send today)
- **Trust signals:** Shares personal stories, family photos to build connection

## 🎨 UI/UX Preferences

### Visual Priorities
1. **Large touch targets:** Often uses phone while holding baby/cooking
2. **High contrast:** Works in varying lighting conditions (bright daylight, dim evening)
3. **Minimal text:** Prefers icons, images over long instructions
4. **Green color scheme:** Associates green with success/profit (culturally positive)

### Interaction Preferences
- **Voice-first:** Types when necessary, prefers voice notes for longer messages
- **One-tap actions:** Quick confirmations for routine tasks
- **Swipe gestures:** Swipe right to approve, left to dismiss
- **Haptic feedback:** Confirmation vibrations for critical actions

### Accessibility Needs
- **Bangla font size:** Minimum 16px for readability
- **Offline mode:** Works during internet outages (common in Mirpur)
- **Data saver:** Limited mobile data budget (৳ 500-800/month)

## ⚠️ Risk Factors & Mitigation

### Adoption Risks
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|------------|--------|---------------------|
| Tech overwhelm | Medium | High | Simplified onboarding, video tutorials in Bengali |
| Trust in AI decisions | Medium | Medium | Show confidence scores, allow manual override |
| Data privacy concerns | Low | High | Clear privacy policy, local data storage option |
| Internet dependency | High | Medium | Offline-first design, sync when online |

### Business Risks
- **Over-reliance on platform:** Diversify across FB, Instagram, WhatsApp
- **Courier service disruptions:** Multi-courier integration (Pathao + Steadfast + RedX)
- **Payment fraud:** AI-powered fraud detection, manual verification for large orders

## 📝 User Stories

### Story 1: Order from WhatsApp
```
As Rina, I want to:
- Receive a WhatsApp message: "Apu, red dress ta ৳ 450 e niben? Delivery address: Mirpur-10..."
- Have Shothik automatically extract: product, price, address, customer name
- Show me a pre-filled order card for confirmation
- Book Pathao courier with one tap after I confirm
- Send tracking number to customer via WhatsApp automatically

So that I can process orders 5x faster without manual copy-paste errors.
```

### Story 2: Payment Verification
```
As Rina, I want to:
- Receive bKash SMS: "You received ৳ 450 from Rahima. Balance ৳ 12,340"
- Have Shothik match this payment to pending order #1234
- Mark order as "Paid" automatically
- Update my daily ledger
- Send thank you message to customer

So that I don't have to manually check and match 20+ payments daily.
```

### Story 3: Unpaid Order Follow-up
```
As Rina, I want to:
- Get notified: "Order #1240 delivered 3 days ago, payment still pending"
- See suggested polite reminder message in Bangla/Banglish
- Tap "Send Reminder" to WhatsApp the customer
- If no response in 24 hours, escalate to phone call reminder

So that I can recover unpaid amounts without awkward manual follow-ups.
```
