## 😫 Pain Points & Frustrations

### Critical Pain Points (Daily)
| Pain Point | Frequency | Impact | Current Workaround |
|------------|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| Literature review (finding relevant papers) | Daily | High (time-consuming, scattered sources) | Google Scholar alerts, manual arXiv browsing |
| PDF management (organization, annotation) | Daily | Medium (hundreds of PDFs, hard to search) | Zotero + manual folder structure |
| Citation management | Daily | High (error-prone, format inconsistencies) | Zotero/Mendeley, manual BibTeX editing |
| Writing in LaTeX | Daily | Medium (compilation errors, version control issues) | Overleaf + Git, frequent backups |
| Data preprocessing for experiments | Weekly | High (messy data, inconsistent formats) | Custom Python scripts, Jupyter notebooks |

### Weekly Pain Points
- Responding to reviewer comments (rejection rates 70-80% at top venues)
- Grant proposal writing (complex forms, tight deadlines)
- Student progress meetings (tracking individual milestones)
- Peer review requests (unpaid, time-consuming but necessary for reputation)

### Monthly Pain Points
- Journal submission processes (multiple portals, formatting requirements)
- Budget tracking for research grants
- Equipment procurement (import restrictions, customs delays)

## ⚠️ Risk Factors & Mitigation

### Adoption Risks
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|------------|--------|---------------------|
| Skepticism about AI writing | High | Medium | Emphasize AI as assistant, not replacement; show citation sources |
| Data privacy concerns | Medium | High | Local processing option, no data sharing without consent |
| Over-reliance on AI suggestions | Medium | Medium | Show confidence scores, require human verification for citations |
| Integration with existing tools | High | Medium | Support Zotero, Mendeley, Overleaf exports/imports |

### Academic Risks
- **Plagiarism accusations:** Ensure all AI-generated text is properly cited/flagged
- **Reviewer bias against AI-assisted papers:** Transparency about AI usage in acknowledgments
- **Quality degradation:** Human-in-the-loop for all critical sections (methodology, conclusions)

## 📝 User Stories

### Story 1: Literature Review Acceleration
```
As Dr. Rahman, I want to:
- Upload 50 PDFs on "NLP for low-resource languages"
- Have Shothik extract key concepts, methodologies, findings from each
- Generate a structured literature review matrix (authors, methods, datasets, results)
- Identify research gaps and suggest novel contributions
- Export to BibTeX with proper categorization

So that I can complete literature reviews in 2 hours instead of 2 days.
```

### Story 2: Citation Verification
```
As Dr. Rahman, I want to:
- Write a paper section with 20+ citations
- Have Shothik verify each citation against the original source
- Flag any misattributions, incorrect page numbers, or taken-out-of-context quotes
- Suggest additional relevant citations I may have missed
- Auto-format bibliography to target journal style (IEEE, APA, ACM)

So that I avoid embarrassing citation errors that reviewers criticize.
```

### Story 3: Experiment Documentation
```
As Dr. Rahman, I want to:
- Run Python experiments in Jupyter notebooks
- Have Shothik automatically log hyperparameters, datasets, results
- Generate experiment comparison tables and visualizations
- Write methodology section draft based on experiment logs
- Save everything in reproducible format (Docker container, requirements.txt)

So that my students can reproduce experiments and I can write papers faster.
```
