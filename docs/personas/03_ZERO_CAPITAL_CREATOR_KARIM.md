## 🎯 Goals & Motivations

### Primary Goals
1. **Reach Monetization Threshold:** 1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours (YouTube)
2. **Diversify Income Streams:** Ad revenue + sponsorships + fan donations + affiliate marketing
3. **Build Personal Brand:** Become recognized tech reviewer in Bangladesh
4. **Achieve Financial Stability:** Consistent ৳ 50,000+/month income

### Secondary Goals
- Collaborate with bigger creators for cross-promotion
- Expand to multiple platforms (YouTube, Facebook Watch, TikTok Creator Fund)
- Invest in better equipment (gimbal, microphone, lighting)
- Hire part-time editor once revenue stabilizes

### Emotional Drivers
- **Creative Freedom:** Express opinions, review products honestly
- **Social Recognition:** Fame, fan interactions, brand invitations
- **Family Expectations:** Prove content creation is a "real career"
- **Escape Traditional Jobs:** Avoid 9-5 office work, value flexibility

## 😫 Pain Points & Frustrations

### Critical Pain Points (Daily)
| Pain Point | Frequency | Impact | Current Workaround |
|------------|-----------|--------|-------------------|
| Idea generation (what to create next) | Daily | High (creative block, trending topics miss) | Browse YouTube/FB trends manually, copy successful formats |
| Script writing | Daily | Medium (time-consuming, writer's block) | Rough notes in phone, improvise while recording |
| Video editing | Daily | High (2-3 hours per 10-min video) | CapCut/KineMaster on phone, basic cuts/transitions |
| Thumbnail design | Per video | Medium (poor CTR if unattractive) | Canva templates, sometimes pays Fiverr freelancer ৳ 200-500 |
| Title/description optimization | Per video | High (algorithm visibility) | Guesswork, copy competitors' titles |

### Weekly Pain Points
- Analytics review (understanding what works, what doesn't)
- Responding to comments (engagement boosts algorithm)
- Reaching out to brands for sponsorships (low response rate)
- Managing multiple platform uploads (different formats, requirements)

### Monthly Pain Points
- Income volatility stress (some months ৳ 10k, some months ৳ 50k)
- Equipment upgrades (saving for better camera, mic)
- Copyright strikes (background music, fair use disputes)
- Platform policy changes (demonetization risks)

## 📱 Technology Stack

### Daily Apps
| App | Usage | Pain Points |
|-----|-------|-------------|

| Google Trends | 15 min/day (topic research) | Not localized for Bangladesh trends |

### Desired Features
- **Trending Topic Alerts:** Bangladesh-specific viral topics before they peak
- **AI Script Assistant:** Generate script outlines from topic ideas
- **Auto-Thumbnail Generator:** High-CTR thumbnails from video frames
- **Revenue Estimator:** Predict earnings before publishing
- **Multi-Platform Scheduler:** One-click publish to YouTube, FB, TikTok

## 🔄 Daily Workflow

### Morning (10:00 AM - 1:00 PM)
```
10:00 AM → Wake up (late nights from editing)
10:30 AM → Check overnight analytics, comments
11:00 AM → Research trending topics (YouTube, FB, TikTok)
12:00 PM → Plan today's content (1-2 videos)
1:00 PM  → Breakfast + family time
```

### Afternoon (2:00 PM - 6:00 PM)
```
2:00 PM  → Film content (product reviews, vlogs)
3:30 PM  → Lunch break
4:00 PM  → Edit video #1 (CapCut on phone)
5:30 PM  → Create thumbnail, write title/description
6:00 PM  → Upload to YouTube (schedule for prime time)
```

### Evening (7:00 PM - 12:00 AM)
```
7:00 PM  → Dinner + rest
8:00 PM  → Edit video #2 (if filming two today)
9:30 PM  → Cross-post to Facebook, TikTok
10:00 PM → Respond to comments (engagement boost)
11:00 PM → Research tomorrow's topics
12:00 AM → Sleep
```

## 💬 Language Patterns

### Common Phrases (Bangla/Banglish/English Mix)
- "Aajke kon topic e video banabo?" (What topic for today's video?)
- "Views kom esheche" (Views are low)
- "Thumbnail ta change korte hobe" (Need to change thumbnail)
- "Sponsor pawa jacche na" (Not getting sponsors)
- "Algorithm bujhte pari na" (Can't understand the algorithm)
- "Subscriber barano kothin" (Growing subscribers is hard)
- "bKash e taka pathao" (Send money via bKash)

### Communication Style
- **Casual, conversational:** Talks to camera like talking to friends
- **Energetic, expressive:** Hand gestures, facial expressions for engagement
- **Code-switching:** Mixes Bengali + English tech terms ("battery backup", "performance")
- **Call-to-action heavy:** "Like, comment, share", "Subscribe korun"

## 🎨 UI/UX Preferences

### Visual Priorities
1. **Mobile-first design:** 95% workflow on smartphone
2. **Vertical video optimization:** TikTok/Reels/Shorts format support
3. **Bright, bold colors:** Eye-catching thumbnails, high contrast
4. **Emoji-rich:** Uses emojis in titles, descriptions, comments

### Interaction Preferences
- **Swipe gestures:** Quick navigation between videos, analytics
- **One-tap actions:** Publish, boost, respond to comments
- **Voice commands:** Hands-free editing while reviewing footage
- **Real-time previews:** See changes instantly (thumbnails, titles)

### Accessibility Needs
- **Low data usage:** Compresses videos for upload on mobile data
- **Offline editing:** Works during internet outages
- **Battery optimization:** Long editing sessions drain phone battery

## ⚠️ Risk Factors & Mitigation

### Adoption Risks
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|------------|--------|---------------------|
| Skepticism about AI creativity | Medium | Medium | Position AI as tool, not replacement; show successful examples |
| Cost sensitivity | High | High | Freemium model, free tier sufficient for basic needs |
| Learning curve | Low | Medium | Video tutorials in Bengali, in-app guided tours |
| Internet dependency | Medium | Medium | Offline editing mode, sync when online |

### Business Risks
- **Platform dependency:** Diversify across YouTube, FB, TikTok to reduce risk
- **Copyright issues:** Provide copyright-free music, image libraries
- **Income volatility:** Offer financial planning tools, emergency fund guidance

## 📝 User Stories

### Story 1: Viral Topic Discovery
```
As Karim, I want to:
- Open Shothik app and see: "Trending now: Budget smartphone under ৳ 20,000"
- See predicted views: 15,000-25,000 if published within 24 hours
- Get script outline: Intro, specs comparison, camera test, battery test, verdict
- See competitor videos on same topic (and their performance)
- Get title suggestions: "সেরা বাজেট ফোন ২০২৫?" vs "২০ হাজারের মধ্যে স্মার্টফোন!"

So that I can create timely content that rides the trend wave before it peaks.
```

### Story 2: Thumbnail Optimization
```
As Karim, I want to:
- Upload my video to Shothik
- Have AI analyze 10 frames and suggest best thumbnail candidates
- See A/B test predictions: "Thumbnail A: 7% CTR, Thumbnail B: 9% CTR"
- Customize with text overlays, emojis, arrows
- Download or directly publish to YouTube

So that I can maximize click-through rates without paying a designer.
```

### Story 3: Revenue Diversification
```
As Karim, I want to:
- See breakdown: Ad revenue ৳ 12k, Sponsorships ৳ 8k, Donations ৳ 5k, Affiliates ৳ 3k
- Get suggestions: "Your tech review audience would convert well on Daraz affiliate program"
- See projected income if I reach 10k subs: ৳ 60k-80k/month
- Get tips: "Post 2 videos/week instead of 1 to increase ad revenue by 40%"

So that I can plan my content strategy around income goals, not just views.
```
