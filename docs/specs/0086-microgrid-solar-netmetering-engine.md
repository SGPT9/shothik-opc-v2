# Specification 0086: Autonomous Micro-Grid Solar Telemetry, Net-Metering Settlement & Rural Palli Bidyut Energy Tokenization Engine

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Context
Access to continuous, reliable electrical power is a make-or-break operational requirement for small manufacturing workshops, Nilkhet 300-DPI offset printing presses, decentralized agro-cold storages, dark store fulfillment hubs, and rural cottage enterprises across Bangladesh. Over 35 million consumers and rural businesses are served by the Bangladesh Rural Electrification Board (বাংলাদেশ পল্লী বিদ্যুতায়ন বোর্ড - BREB / পল্লী বিদ্যুৎ) across 80+ Palli Bidyut Samity (PBS) regional zones.

Rural and peri-urban small enterprises experience regular seasonal load-shedding and high commercial peak utility tariffs (৳12–৳18 per kWh). To mitigate this, businesses are adopting rooftop solar photovoltaics (PV), lithium-ion battery energy storage systems (BESS), and diesel backup generators. Under the **SREDA (Sustainable and Renewable Energy Development Authority)** and **BERC (Bangladesh Energy Regulatory Commission) Net-Metering Guidelines 2018 (revised 2023)**, commercial consumers can connect bi-directional smart meters to the grid, export surplus solar power during daylight hours, and offset their monthly electricity bill.

Slice 86 implements an autonomous energy telemetry, net-metering settlement, genset hybrid optimization, and SREDA green carbon offset engine.

### 2.2 Bi-Directional Smart Net-Meter Telemetry
- **Solar Generation (kWh)**: Output generated by solar PV string inverters.
- **Facility Load (kWh)**: Total energy consumed by equipment, motors, and lighting.
- **Grid Export (kWh)**: Surplus power pushed back into the national grid when Solar Generation > Facility Load.
- **Grid Import (kWh)**: Energy drawn from the national grid when Facility Load > Solar Generation.
- **Battery SOC (%)**: Battery state of charge (20% to 100%).
- **Inverter Temperature (°C)**: Thermal safety monitoring (alert if $>65^\circ\text{C}$).

### 2.3 Net-Metering Settlement & Rollover Tokenization
- **Net Units Formula**:
  $$\text{Net Billable Units (kWh)} = \max(0, \text{Grid Import (kWh)} - \text{Grid Export (kWh)})$$
- **Surplus Rollover Credits**: If $\text{Grid Export} > \text{Grid Import}$, credit units are tokenized and banked for future seasonal billing cycles under BERC Section 14.
- **Monthly Bill Settlement**:
  $$\text{Total Bill (BDT)} = (\text{Net Units} \times \text{Tariff Rate}) + \text{Demand Charge} + 5\% \text{ Statutory VAT}$$

### 2.4 Diesel Genset vs. Solar Hybrid Arbitrage
- Diesel fuel cost in Bangladesh: ৳108.00 per liter.
- Genset generation rate: ~3.2 kWh per liter of diesel $\rightarrow$ **৳33.75 per kWh** generation cost.
- Solar + Battery Levelized Cost of Energy (LCOE): ~**৳7.20 per kWh**.
- Automated Dispatch Rule: Whenever grid power drops (load-shedding event), prioritize Battery BESS discharge down to 25% SOC before triggering the diesel genset starter, saving up to 78% in fuel expenditure.

### 2.5 SREDA Statutory Green Carbon Offset Certificate
- Avoided Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Factor for Bangladesh National Grid: **$0.62\text{ kg } CO_2\text{ per kWh}$** of clean solar generation (SREDA Grid Emission Factor 2023).
- Generates cryptographic SHA-256 carbon reduction seal for sustainability compliance, ESG disclosures, and green loan concessions.
