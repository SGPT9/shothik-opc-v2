## 2. Complete Categorized Feature Matrix

### 🎙️ Category 1: ChatGPT Omni Voice & Multimodal Intelligence
1. **ChatGPT Omni-Style Realtime Voice Loop** ([`omni-voice-executive.ts`](file:///c:/Users/USER/hermes/shothik-web-mirror-v2/artifacts/shothik/src/lib/infrastructure/omni-voice-executive.ts)): Continuous hands-free voice conversations in natural speech.
2. **Sub-50ms Voice Activity Interruption (Silero VAD)**: Interrupt the voice AI mid-sentence; VAD pauses audio in <50ms.
3. **Banglish & Multilingual ASR (`buzz-whisper-service.ts`)**: Transcribes mixed English + Bengali (`"আজকের bKash sales report দেখাও"`), Hindi, and Spanish dictation.
4. **Open-LLM-VTuber 3D Avatar Presenter (`vtuber-presenter.ts`)**: 3D Live2D/VRM avatar that narrates pitch decks and answers Q&A live on trackable links.
5. **Real-Time Visual Camera & Screen Share Perception**: Analyzes live camera feeds and screen shares to critique code, UI layouts, and diagrams.
6. **Early Morning Executive Briefing**: Compiles overnight task logs, bKash revenue, and calendar schedules into a spoken morning audio report.

---

### 🛍️ Category 2: F-Commerce & Autonomous Commerce
7. **🤖 Live Banglish Price Bargaining AI (`sales-negotiation-courier.ts`)**: Negotiates live with bargaining customers on Messenger/WhatsApp based on founder's minimum price floor (*"১৩৫০ টাকায় দেওয়া যাবে (% ছাড়)"*).
8. **💳 In-WhatsApp bKash / Nagad Direct Payment Cards**: Generates dynamic in-chat payment links with 1-tap mobile app deep-linking (`https://pay.bkash.com.bd/...`).
9. **⚡ SMS TrxID Verification & Auto-Receipts (`bkash-nagad-adapter.ts`)**: Verifies 10-character `TrxID` numbers instantly against merchant SMS webhooks and issues PDF receipts.
10. **🚚 1-Click Steadfast / Pathao Courier Parcel Booking**: Automatically calls Steadfast / Pathao / RedX Courier API upon payment, books parcel pickup, and DMs tracking links (`BD_STEADFAST_755448`) on WhatsApp!
11. **📱 Facebook & Instagram Reel Comment-to-DM Engine (`openreply` & `ChatbotX`)**: DMs product catalogs and payment links to anyone commenting *"Price?"* on social posts after verifying follow status.

---

### 📖 Category 3: 3-Fold Writing Studio & Creative Authoring
12. **Replit 3-Fold Writing Workspace (`ReplitBookWorkspace.tsx`)**: Fold 1: Chapter Tree; Fold 2: 5-Mode Book Canvas; Fold 3: Polsia Swarm console.
13. **🔮 InkOS 3-Scenario Narrative Forecasts (`inkos`)**: Generates 2-5 alternative plot futures (beats, character decisions, risks) in `BookCenterCanvas.tsx` before locking chapter text.
14. **Character & Worldbuilding Canon Vault**: Tracks character traits, locations, and open plot threads across 100+ chapters.
15. **📚 Multi-Chapter EPUB Ebook & PDF Exporter (`epubPdfExporter.ts`)**: Generates downloadable EPUB ebooks, Markdown manuscripts, and styled HTML documents.

---

### 📄 Category 4: Document & Spreadsheet Workspace
16. **📄 GenOffice Byte-Exact DOCX Exporter (`genoffice`)**: Modifies only dirty paragraphs in `.docx` OOXML XML, keeping original layout, comments, and styles byte-for-byte intact when opened in Word.
17. **📊 GenOffice Rust-Powered XLSX Engine (`genoffice`)**: High-performance `.xlsx` computation sidecar in Rust (`IronCalc`) with pivot tables, slicers, and formula tracing in `SheetArtifact.tsx`.
18. **📊 3-Scenario Monte Carlo Financial Modeling (`ScenarioLabModal.tsx`)**: 100-run simulation matrix calculating Base Case, Aggressive Growth, and Conservative Risk scenarios.
19. **🔗 Papermark Trackable Investor Link Sharing (`papermark`)**: Generates trackable pitch deck URLs (`https://papermark.shothik.app/...`) with page-by-page view analytics.

---

### 🌐 Category 5: Keyless Web Search & Data Scraping
20. **🌐 Wigolo Keyless Web Search Engine (`wigolo-service.ts`)**: Local-first rank-fusion web search and page crawling into `DeepResearchArtifact.tsx` with **zero API key fees**.
21. **🕸️ ScrapeGraphAI Structured Data Extraction (`Scrapegraph-ai`)**: Scrapes competitor sites and pricing grids into structured tables in `SheetArtifact.tsx`.
22. **📚 Academic Literature Research**: Pulls peer-reviewed papers from PubMed, bioRxiv, arXiv, and OpenAlex with IEEE/APA citation formatting.

---

### 🎟️ Category 6: Promotions, Referrals & Loyalty Engine
23. **🎟️ OfferKit Dynamic Discount Engine (`offerkit`)**: Generates 1-time personalized promo codes (`DESI-10-OFF`) for live chat price negotiations.
24. **👥 Dual-Reward Referral Generator**: Generates `{PREFIX}-{code}` referral links awarding dual conversion rewards (bKash cashback for referrer + discount for referee).
25. **🔐 Atomic Stackable Redemptions**: Applies multiple promo codes and gift card ledgers to orders with zero partial failures.

---

### 🤖 Category 7: Web & Browser Automation
26. **🌐 ego-lite Parallel Browser Spaces (`ego-lite`)**: Runs background web tasks (LinkedIn posts, Stripe payouts) in parallel browser spaces without stealing screen focus.
27. **🍪 Chrome Profile Cookie Inheritance**: Agents inherit logged-in sessions (GitHub, Vercel, Twitter) with zero login script setup.
28. **🤖 Alibaba PageAgent In-Page JS Auto-Pilot (`page-agent`)**: Converts 20-click admin forms and canvas workflows into single natural language voice/text prompts.

### 🔌 Category 8: Cloud Infrastructure, Integrations & Security
29. **🔌 Pipedream Connect Zero-Secret OAuth Vault (`pipedream-connect.ts`)**: Connects to 2,000+ SaaS apps (Google Calendar, Slack, HubSpot, Notion) without touching raw OAuth tokens.
