- **Shothik Advantage**: Polsia OPC handles live price bargaining, verifies bKash/Nagad payments, and automatically books Steadfast/Pathao parcel pick-up via API.

### 🏆 Pillar 2: Replit 3-Fold Writing Studio & Canvas

- **Shothik Advantage**: Integrated Gortex Skill Vault (`.agents/skills/`), peer review forums, star transactions ([`SendCreditsButton.tsx`](file:///c:/Users/USER/hermes/shothik-web-mirror-v2/artifacts/shothik/src/components/credits/SendCreditsButton.tsx)), and community-contributed prompt playbooks.
