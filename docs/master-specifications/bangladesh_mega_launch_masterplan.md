## 1. Core Positioning: "The No-Employee Business Engine"

Generic AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude) fail in Bangladesh because they only generate text. Shothik AI wins because **it executes end-to-end business actions**:

```
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │                      THE SHOTHIK BANGLADESH ADVANTAGE                       │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  Customer Bargains ──► AI Negotiates Price ──► Verifies bKash ──► Books Courier
  ("১২০০ টাকায় হবে?")   (SalesNegotiation)     (TrxID Ledger)     (Steadfast/Pathao)
```

1. **AI Negotiates Price Live**: Handles price bargaining (*"১২০০ টাকায় হবে?"*) within the founder's minimum price floor.
2. **Verifies bKash / Nagad TrxID**: Automatically validates payment SMS receipts in real time.
3. **Books Courier Parcel**: Calls Steadfast/Pathao/RedX API to book parcel delivery and DMs tracking links on WhatsApp.
4. **Zero Extra Employee Cost**: Replaces the need for 3 full-time chat admins.
