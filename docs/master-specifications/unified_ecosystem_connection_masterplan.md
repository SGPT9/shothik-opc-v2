## 2. The 5 Connected Pillars & Their Architectural Interlocks

### 💬 Pillar 1: The 3 Chat Modes
1. **Mode 1: Cofounder Mode (`ChatAgentPage.tsx`)**:
   - Polsia AI Cofounder executing full-stack software, document editing, and F-Commerce sales bargaining ([`sales-negotiation-courier.ts`](file:///c:/Users/USER/hermes/shothik-web-mirror-v2/artifacts/shothik/src/lib/infrastructure/sales-negotiation-courier.ts)).
2. **Mode 2: Web Search Mode (`wigolo-service.ts`)**:
   - Keyless local-first web search and structured web scraping without external API key fees.
3. **Mode 3: Scenario Lab Mode (`scenario-lab-engine.ts`)**:
   - **Primary Chat Monetization Engine!** Runs 900-run Monte Carlo simulations over financial models, product launches, and operational risks. Generating a full forecast report costs **5 Credits**.

---

### 🎙️ Pillar 2: Voice Agent Tiering (Free vs. Paid Benefits)
- **Free Tier**: 5 voice minutes/day with standard speech recognition and basic conversational turns.
- **Paid Benefits Tier** (Unlocked via Pro Subscription or Credit Balance >100):
  - Unlimited voice turns.
  - Sub-50ms Silero VAD speech interruption.
  - Open-LLM-VTuber 3D Avatar narration ([`vtuber-presenter.ts`](file:///c:/Users/USER/hermes/shothik-web-mirror-v2/artifacts/shothik/src/lib/infrastructure/vtuber-presenter.ts)).
  - Early Morning Executive Briefings ([`omni-voice-executive.ts`](file:///c:/Users/USER/hermes/shothik-web-mirror-v2/artifacts/shothik/src/lib/infrastructure/omni-voice-executive.ts)).
  - Direct WhatsApp authentication & bKash payment triggers.

---

### 🧠 Pillar 3: Second-Me Twin Agent (Task Credit Engine)
- **Concept**: The user's Second-Me Twin Agent acts as their persistent autonomous alter-ego operating in the background.
- **Task Credit Metering**:
  - Every dispatched background job (overnight market analysis, competitor price scraping, code refactoring) requires **Task Credits** ([`twin/types.ts`](file:///c:/Users/USER/hermes/shothik-web-mirror-v2/artifacts/shothik/src/app-next/%28primary-layout%29/twin/types.ts#L89)).
  - Small Task: 5 Task Credits | Medium Task: 12 Task Credits | Complex Overnight Task: 20 Task Credits.

---

### 🌐 Pillar 4: Community Forum (`/community`) & VC Flywheel
As documented in [`community-playbook.md`](file:///c:/Users/USER/hermes/shothik-web-mirror-v2/docs/community-playbook.md), the community operates an **Autonomous Hybrid Network**:
- **3 Channel Modes**: `agent_only` (AI swarm dialogue), `human_only` (private founder reviews), and `both` (open forum).
- **Dual Flywheel**:
  - 1-Click Share: Users share live Scenario Lab Monte Carlo models and Marp pitch decks to `/community`.
  - Public URLs attract external visitors, who click **"Fork Artifact"** to bring the project into their 3-Fold IDE workspace.
  - Forking and running AI actions consumes **Credits** and drives adoption of **Polsia OPC paid company slots ($15/mo)**.
