## 🚀 Scenario 1: The Solo Tech Founder (Dhaka / Singapore)
**User**: Tanvir, Solo Founder building an AI Logistics SaaS for South Asia  
**Goal**: Launch product, secure seed funding, and automate customer acquisition without hiring employees.

```
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │                     SCENARIO 1: SOLO TECH FOUNDER                           │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  1. Hands-Free Prompt  ──►  2. Canvas Artifact Generation  ──►  3. Automated Growth
  (Voice / Code-Mixed)       (GenOffice Docs/Xlsx/Pptx)          (Papermark + OpenReply)
```

### End-to-End Workflow Execution:
1. **Hands-Free Idea Validation (00:00 - 01:30)**:
   - Tanvir opens Shothik on his phone while commuting and speaks in Bengali+English dictation (`Composer.tsx`): *"Validate my logistics SaaS idea for Dhaka & Chittagong ports and build a 3-scenario financial model"*.
   - **Buzz Whisper** transcribes speech; **Wigolo Engine** performs keyless web research on Bangladesh shipping data; **ScrapeGraphAI** extracts current freight rates into a structured table.
2. **Interactive Pitch Deck & Financial Sheet (01:30 - 03:00)**:
   - Shothik opens the **Canvas Panel** showing `SlidesArtifact.tsx` (16:9 Marp deck) and `SheetArtifact.tsx` (Monte Carlo model).
   - Tanvir uses **PageAgent** voice auto-pilot: *"Set aggressive growth target to 35% ARR"*. PageAgent updates the formula inputs live on screen.
   - **GenOffice Docs** exports the final business plan to a byte-exact `.docx` file.
3. **Investor Outreach & Social Viral Loop (03:00 - 05:00)**:
   - Tanvir clicks **`[ 🔗 Trackable Link ]`** in `SlidesArtifact.tsx` to generate a **Papermark** investor link (`https://papermark.shothik.app/view/deck_logistics`).
   - He posts a 15-second teaser Reel on Instagram. **OpenReply** automatically monitors comments: when an angel investor comments `"DECK"`, OpenReply verifies their follow status and DMs the Papermark link.
   - **ChatbotX** broadcasts the pitch deck to Tanvir's WhatsApp investor contact list.
   - **ego-lite** runs in a parallel browser space to post the launch announcement on LinkedIn and ProductHunt automatically.

## 🛍️ Scenario 2: The E-Commerce Brand Owner (Sylhet / London)
**User**: Anika, E-Commerce Brand Owner selling handcrafted sustainable fashion  
**Goal**: Expand sales from local Instagram orders to global cross-border sales with automated customer service.

```
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │                   SCENARIO 2: E-COMMERCE BRAND OWNER                        │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  1. Playbook Intake   ──►  2. Omnichannel Support Agent  ──►  3. Live VTuber Pitch
  (Gortex Book-to-Skill)    (ChatbotX + Evolution API)        (Papermark + Silero VAD)
```

### End-to-End Workflow Execution:
1. **Playbook-to-Agent Skill Distillation (00:00 - 01:00)**:
   - Anika uploads her 150-page Brand & Customer Service Playbook PDF into Shothik.
   - **Gortex** distills the PDF into an executable skill (`.agents/skills/sustainable-fashion-playbook/SKILL.md`) achieving **50x token savings**.
2. **Omnichannel Customer Support & Lead Automation (01:00 - 03:00)**:
   - Polsia AI Cofounder assumes the brand persona and handles customer inquiries 24/7 across **WhatsApp**, **Instagram DMs**, and **Telegram** via **ChatbotX**.
   - When a customer asks for pricing on WhatsApp, ChatbotX sends a dynamic product catalog link generated via **InsForge BaaS**.
3. **Interactive Investor & Wholesale Presenter (03:00 - 05:00)**:
   - Anika sends a Wholesale Catalog link to a London distributor.
   - The distributor opens the link and meets **Open-LLM-VTuber Presenter** (`vtuber-presenter.ts`). The 3D avatar narrates product lines in natural English.
   - When the distributor interrupts mid-sentence (*"What's your minimum order quantity?"*), **Silero VAD** pauses narration in <50ms and answers accurately using **Mem0 RAG Memory**.

## 📚 Scenario 3: The Fiction & Screenplay Author (Toronto / Tokyo)
**User**: Marcus, Author writing a sci-fi novel & interactive branching game  
**Goal**: Write a 80,000-word manuscript, resolve plot holes, and export EPUB/PDF editions.

```
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │                   SCENARIO 3: FICTION & SCREENPLAY AUTHOR                   │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  1. 3-Fold Writing Studio ──► 2. Narrative Forecasts  ──► 3. Multilingual EPUB
  (ReplitBookWorkspace.tsx)    (InkOS Branching Engine)    (GenOffice PDF Reflow)
```

### End-to-End Workflow Execution:
1. **3-Fold Writing Workspace (00:00 - 02:00)**:
   - Marcus opens `/writing-studio?mode=3fold` (`ReplitBookWorkspace.tsx`).
   - **Fold 1** displays Chapter Tree & word count analytics; **Fold 2** presents `BookCenterCanvas.tsx`; **Fold 3** displays Polsia Swarm logs.
2. **InkOS 3-Scenario Narrative Forecast (02:00 - 03:30)**:
   - Marcus reaches Chapter 12 and hesitates on a major plot turning point.
   - He clicks **`[ 🔮 Narrative Forecast ]`** powered by **InkOS**. The engine generates 3 isolated plot futures:
     - *Branch A*: Hero aligns with rebellion (Risk: High pacing tension).
     - *Branch B*: Hero turns double agent (Risk: Character motivation split).
     - *Branch C*: Unexpected portal anomaly (Risk: Worldbuilding rule break).
   - InkOS checks **Character & Worldbuilding Canon** to ensure no continuity errors occur. Marcus selects Branch B.
3. **EPUB & Word Document Export (03:30 - 05:00)**:
   - Marcus exports the complete manuscript using **epubPdfExporter.ts**.
   - **GenOffice Docs** generates a byte-exact `.docx` file for his publisher and an EPUB file for Kindle distribution.

**Goal**: Perform competitor audits, generate white-label pitch decks, and deploy client web apps.


2. **Pipedream Managed OAuth & CRM Sync (01:30 - 03:00)**:
   - Sarah connects HubSpot and Slack via **Pipedream Connect** ([`/connectors`](file:///c:/Users/USER/hermes/shothik-web-mirror-v2/artifacts/shothik/src/app-next/%28primary-layout%29/connectors/page.tsx)) with zero API key exposure.

3. **Dokploy/Coolify 1-Click Container Deployment (03:00 - 05:00)**:
