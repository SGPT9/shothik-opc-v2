# 🥁 Writing Beats Skill

Use this skill when planning, outlining, or editing chapter beats inside `/writing-studio` or `/twin`.

## Core Beat Principles

1. **Every Scene Has a Shift**:
   - A beat is a unit of change. Every scene must shift the emotional state, value, or information state from positive to negative or vice versa (+ to - or - to +).

2. **Beat Breakdown Structure**:
   - **Inciting Trigger**: What starts the scene motion?
   - **Progressive Complications**: Obstacles or unexpected responses.
   - **Crisis Point**: Dilemma or decision required by the character.
   - **Climax**: Action taken in response to the crisis.
   - **Resolution / Sequel**: Reflection or immediate consequence setting up the next beat.

3. **Pacing Rules**:
   - Short, punchy beats increase tension and velocity.
   - Longer, reflective beats deepen character interiority and worldbuilding.
